Novagram is a fast, secure messenger for macOS — built for people who want more from their conversations.

Send messages, photos, videos and files of any type, make voice and video calls, create groups and channels, and stay in sync across all your devices. Everything is fast, reliable, and private.

Novagram adds a set of power-user tools on top — all included, no subscription, no paywall:

• Ghost Mode — Read messages and stay online without sending a single "seen", "typing" or "last seen" signal.

• Multi-Account — Add several accounts and switch between them in one click. Keep your active accounts live in the background so you never miss a notification.

• Chat Lock (PIN) — Protect individual chats with a PIN code. Only you can open a locked chat, even if someone else uses your Mac.

• Voice → Text — Convert any voice message to text right on your device.

• Instant Translation — Translate any incoming message with a single click, in any language.

• Auto-Text — Automatically add a custom signature to the end of every outgoing message.

• Edited Message History — See every previous version of an edited message.

• QR Code Login — Sign in by scanning a QR code straight from the number screen — no SMS code typing needed.

Every feature can be turned on or off in Settings. Novagram is designed to feel native on macOS — full keyboard shortcuts, menu bar integration, multi-window support, and the speed you expect from a real desktop app.

Download Novagram and take control of your conversations.
